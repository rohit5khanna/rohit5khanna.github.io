About

I'm a researcher who likes problems where rigorous math collides with noisy, real-world data — and the modeling has to hold up anyway.

Currently an MS candidate in Operations Research & Industrial Engineering at UT Austin, finishing a thesis on the mathematical limits of quantile-parameterized distributions and a new method, QFlex, for modeling complex multimodal data.

Before grad school I spent four years as a Senior Geophysicist at ONGC, solving large-scale non-linear inverse problems and reconstructing 3D subsurface models from multi-terabyte seismic data. That grounding in physical modeling and inversion is the lens I bring to machine learning today — whether it's diffusion models for tumor growth or robustness testing for vision transformers.

I care about fundamental research, open science, and modeling that's honest about uncertainty.
